Case 03 · Substrate gap · Larger

The Production-Grade Agent Observability Stack

Archetype: Builder + OptimizerValue tier: $120K–$500K SYNAPSE shareCycle length: ~120 days

Buyer

Engineering organizations whose agent pilots have crossed into production and now confront the Gartner statistic: 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by 2027 — primarily due to lack of production infrastructure. The pain has been crystallized in named industry essays during Feb–Mar 2026.

Offering

Agent-native observability + cost-tracking + eval-running infrastructure. Not a framework; a substrate. Deploys alongside any orchestration layer (LangGraph, CrewAI, custom). Enterprise pricing: $24K–$120K/year per organization.
